# UI copy and layout

- Avoid unnecessary secondary labels and descriptions. Let the layout do the explaining.
- Prefer concise, task-specific labels. Do not add decorative slogans, repeated English eyebrows, or explanatory text that merely restates a visible heading or obvious control.
- Keep secondary guidance only when it helps a concrete decision or prevents a likely mistake; use progressive disclosure for optional details.
- Preserve necessary privacy, storage-loss, security, content-classification, and publication-date caveats. Simplify or consolidate them without hiding important limitations or removing accessible labels.
